## Hacker News — VERIFIED 2026 best practice

Per the May 7, 2026 HN posting guide + recent analyses:

| Optimal | Default |
|---|---|
| **Day:** Tuesday or Thursday | (Wed also fine) |
| **Time (UTC):** 14:00–17:00 | (= 7am-10am PT, 10am-1pm ET) |
| **For you in Davao (PHT, UTC+8):** **10pm Tue night – 1am Wed morning** | |
| Alternative low-competition window: Sunday morning 11am-12pm PT (= 2am-3am Mon morning PHT) | |

**Post format:** "Ask HN" is now considered more effective than "Show HN" for products with existing presence. The pre-written post in `04-hn-and-ih-posts.md` is correct.

**Rules from the May 2026 HN guide (mandatory):**
1. Plain title, no marketing language, no exclamation points, no editorialization
2. Never ask for upvotes from friends — HN detects this and "overheating penalty" applies
3. Reply to comments like a normal person; stay in the thread for 24h
4. **Original source URL** (so for Show HN it should be table-share.org, not a blog post about it)

## After all 10 submitted

Wait 1-2 weeks for approvals. Then:

1. Re-check each — if rejected or stuck, follow up via their contact form
2. Build a backlinks log: keep a spreadsheet of which sites approved you
3. Use `https://ahrefs.com/backlink-checker/` (free) to monitor as backlinks appear in Google's index
4. Each new backlink = a small DR boost in Google's eyes

**Expected outcome after 4-8 weeks:** 5-7 of these will approve. Each one contributes:
- A backlink (transfers authority to your site)
- Direct traffic when the directory's users browse the category (low but steady)
- A second-order effect: when someone searches "csv sharing alternative" + your tool appears on the directory listing, they click through

Reply when you've gotten through them. I'll help with anything specific that bounces back.

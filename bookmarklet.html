How to Use

  1. Drag the blue button to your bookmarks bar
  2. Go to any page with a CSV download (Scale Insights, Helium 10, etc.)
  3. Click the bookmark
  4. It fetches the CSV using YOUR login session and creates a Table Share link
  5. Link copied to clipboard automatically
